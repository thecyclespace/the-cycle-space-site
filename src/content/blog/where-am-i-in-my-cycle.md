The menstrual cycle is not just a countdown to the next period. It's a **rhythm of four phases**, each with its own hormonal pattern, energy and emotional tone.

This tool gives you a **likely guess** of where you are right now in that rhythm — based on the date of your last period and the average length of your cycle. It's not a diagnosis, and it doesn't replace tracking the actual signals your body sends.

<!-- calculator -->

## How does this work?

The tool uses two inputs — the first day of your last period and your average cycle length — to estimate your current **cycle day**, then maps that day to one of four phases:

- **Menstruation** (early days of the cycle)
- **Follicular phase** (after the period, building up to ovulation)
- **Ovulation window** (a few days around the release of an egg)
- **Luteal phase** (after ovulation, before the next period)

The luteal phase is assumed to last roughly **14 days**, which is the typical case but varies between individuals.

## What this tool is — and isn't

- It is a **gentle map** of where you might be in your cycle, useful for self-understanding.
- It is **not a fertility predictor** and not a contraceptive method.
- It is **not a diagnosis**. If your cycles are very irregular, the estimate becomes much less reliable.

The most reliable way to know your phase is to **track your own signals** over several cycles — period dates, basal temperature, cervical mucus, energy and mood. The [1:1 consultation](/services) and the [cycle education session](/services) are designed exactly for that.
