## Why this blog exists

For too long, women's health has been reduced to symptom management. Painful periods? Take a pill. Mood swings? Try this. Irregular cycle? Get on hormonal contraception. The body becomes a problem to suppress, not a system to understand.

The Cycle Space exists because there is another way.

## What you'll find here

- **Cycle education** — what your hormones are actually doing across the four phases, and why it matters.
- **Body literacy** — how to read the signs your body sends, and what they're really saying.
- **Practical tools** — tracking, nutrition, movement and recovery, adapted to where you are in your cycle.
- **Honest writing** — no fear-based wellness, no quick fixes, no vague advice.

## Who this is for

If you've ever been told everything looks normal but you know something is off, you're in the right place. If you've come off the pill and feel disconnected from your own body, you're in the right place. If you're curious, ready to do things differently, and looking for something sustainable — you're in the right place.

> Your body is not a problem to fix. It is a system to understand.
