A simple, private journal to note your basal body temperature, your cervical fluid observation and a few cycle signals — day by day. **Everything stays on your device.** Nothing is sent to a server, nothing is synced, no account is needed.

This tool is here to support **personal observation and learning about your cycle**. It is not a contraceptive method, not a fertility predictor, not a diagnostic tool. It will not tell you whether you are "fertile" or "not fertile" today — that is intentional.

<!-- calculator -->

## A few notes on observation

- **Take your basal temperature** before getting out of bed, ideally at the same time each morning, with the same thermometer.
- **Cervical fluid** is observed throughout the day, not at one fixed time.
- A single data point doesn't say much; **patterns over weeks** start to.
- Cycles vary from one person to another, and from one month to the next. That's normal.

## Why we keep it deliberately simple

Cycle tracking apps often promise predictions and confidence scores. We don't. Predictions can feel reassuring, but they tend to look more accurate than they really are — especially around ovulation, where errors of a few days are common.

This journal stays in the realm of **what you actually observed**, in your own words and numbers. If you'd like a deeper, personalised look at what's happening in your cycle, the [1:1 consultation](/services) is built for that.
