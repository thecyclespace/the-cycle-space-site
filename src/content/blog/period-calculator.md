This tool helps you estimate the date of your next period, your ovulation and your fertile window, from **three simple inputs**: the date of your last period, your usual period duration and your average cycle length.

These are **estimates**. Every cycle is unique, and yours can vary from one month to the next. The longer you track your cycle, the more reliable the estimates become.

<!-- calculator -->

## How does the calculation work?

The calculator uses a simple logic, based on your average cycle length and the fact that the luteal phase (between ovulation and the next period) typically lasts **14 days**:

- **Next period** — last period date + cycle length.
- **End of next period** — next period start + period duration − 1 day.
- **Estimated ovulation** — next period start − 14 days.
- **Fertile window** — 5 days before ovulation and 1 day after (this is when pregnancy is possible).
- **Following cycle** — next period start + cycle length.

## Good to know

- Cycles **vary** from one person to another, and even from one month to the next within the same person.
- **Stress**, **travel**, lifestyle changes, illness or certain hormonal conditions can shift these dates.
- The longer you **track your cycle** over several months (period duration, length, fertility signs), the more personalised the estimates become.
- This tool **does not replace** medical follow-up and **should not be used as a method of contraception**.
